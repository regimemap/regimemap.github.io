Terms

Embed terms, in short

The binding terms, in English, are at regimemap.com/data-api/#embed-terms ↗. This page summarises them.

Free to embed. Any website may show the embeds, commercial or not.

Keep the credit. Keep the credit line under the embed, and its link, visible. The link text is always “Regime Map”; the first word may be in the embed’s language. You may add rel="nofollow" to the link.

Display only. Don’t copy, store or resell the data behind the embeds, and don’t change what they show.

Not legal advice. The embeds show Regime Map’s editorial reading of official sources on the date shown. A lawyer in the country is the authority.
