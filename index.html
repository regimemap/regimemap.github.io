The snippet

This is the whole integration. Paste it where the map should appear.

index.html
<div data-regimemap="map" data-lang="en" data-region="world" data-theme="light">Interactive map: gambling regulation by country.</div>
<p>Map: <a href="https://regimemap.com/gambling-regulation-map/">Regime Map</a></p>
<script async src="https://regimemap.com/embed/v1/embed.js" referrerpolicy="no-referrer"></script>
  • Keep the credit line under the embed. You may add rel="nofollow" to its link.
  • By using the code you accept the embed terms.

What you get

  • Coverage

    More than 100 countries and Northern Ireland

    Five products: online casino, sports betting, poker, lottery and land-based. Lawful exceptions are marked.

  • Languages

    39 languages

    The embed, and the code you paste, come in the reader’s language.

  • Themes

    Light, dark, or follow the reader

    Set once in the code, or let the reader’s system decide.

  • Two forms

    Script or iframe

    Script: Shadow DOM, so host CSS can’t break it and it can’t touch your page. Iframe: for sites that strip scripts.

  • Size

    About 46 KB for the map

    About 8 KB for a card, gzipped. The loader is 2.6 KB, and nothing loads until the embed nears the viewport.

  • Privacy

    No cookies, no tracking

    Nothing stored in the reader’s browser, nothing loaded from any other website, readers’ IP addresses not logged.

  • Accessibility

    Table view, keyboard, contrast themes

    Full keyboard support, 44 px touch targets, reduced motion respected, and it works in Windows contrast themes.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Any website may show the embeds, commercial or not.

What must I keep?

The credit line under the embed and its link, visible. The link text is always “Regime Map”; the word before it may be in the embed’s language.

Can I make the credit link nofollow?

Yes. You may add rel="nofollow" to the credit link.

Does it work on WordPress, or on sites that strip scripts?

On WordPress, paste the code into a Custom HTML block. If your platform removes scripts, choose the iframe form in the configurator: it shows the same embed. Medium and Substack accept neither, so link to the page instead.

Does it set cookies?

No. The embeds set no cookies, store nothing in the reader’s browser and load nothing from any other website. Readers’ IP addresses are not logged.

How current is the data?

The embeds show what Regime Map’s editors have published, as it is published. The map shows the date it was last updated, and each card the date its country was last checked.
